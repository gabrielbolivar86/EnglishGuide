🎨 Today's Colors and Connectors

10 min

Hoy sumamos un quinto color para las palabras pequeñas que conectan y señalan cosas:

Sujeto (¿Quién?) Verbo (Acción) Complemento (Detalle) Conector Preposición / Artículo (nuevo)

Conectores (Repaso)

and = y first = primero then = luego

Conectores Nuevos

because = porque so = así que / por eso also = también

Palabras Pequeñas

a / an = un, una the = el, la, los, las my = mi, mis to = a / para
🔗 ¿Cómo funcionan because, so y also?

because presenta la causa (el porqué):
I eat a quick breakfast because I leave early.

so presenta el resultado (lo que pasa por eso):
I leave early, so I eat a quick breakfast.

also agrega otra acción y va antes del verbo:
She checks documents, and she also writes emails.

✏️ When Does the Verb Change?

15 min

Con he, she, it o una sola persona (my boss, Karen, my mother) el verbo cambia su final. Hay 4 reglas:

1. Regla general: +s

La mayoría de los verbos
cook → cooks work → works

2. Sonidos fuertes: +es

Terminan en -ch, -sh, -s, -x, -z, -o
watch → watches brush → brushes go → goes

3. Consonante + y: -ies

La "y" se cambia por "ies"
study → studies carry → carries

4. Vocal + y: +s

La "y" se queda igual
pray → prays play → plays
My coworker studies English because she wants to travel.

🧠 Natural English vs. Literal Translation

10 min

Algunas frases no se traducen palabra por palabra desde el español:

"Voy al trabajo"

✖ Literal
I go to the work.
✔ Natural
I go to work.
Lugares de rutina fija (work, school, bed) no llevan "the".

"Cocina y limpia"

✖ Confusión común
She cooks an cleans.
✔ Natural
She cooks and cleans.
and une acciones. an va solo antes de una cosa que empieza con sonido de vocal: an apple, an email.

Los dos usos de "to"

📍 Destino (a dónde)
I go to the office.
⚙️ Antes de otro verbo
I get ready to go.
⚡ Nuance Box: Quick vs. Fast
Quick = dura poco tiempo
I take a quick shower. (una ducha corta)
Fast = alta velocidad
He walks fast. (camina rápido)

📖 Read Like the ELA Test

Comprensión lectora

Read the text carefully and answer the multiple-choice questions below.

Lee el texto atentamente y responde las preguntas de opción múltiple.

Karen has a busy routine. First, she gets ready to go to work at 7:30 AM. She prepares a quick breakfast because she leaves early. At the office, she checks important documents, and she also writes emails. She works fast, so she finishes all her tasks before 5:00 PM.

Question 1: Why does Karen prepare a quick breakfast?

A) Because she stays at home.
B) Because she leaves early for work.
C) Because she doesn't cook.

Question 2: What does Karen do at the office in addition to checking documents?

A) She cooks lunch.
B) She also writes emails.
C) She takes a nap.

Question 3: What is the result of Karen working fast?

A) She finishes all her tasks before 5:00 PM.
B) She goes home at 8:00 PM.
C) She reads a book at the office.
